# Proposal

The proposal is these eight sections and nothing else — no cover page, no appendix, no
extra headings.

## 1. The client, and how you reach them

*Who they are and how you contact them — name, organisation, and the channel
you have already used. Two or three lines.*

## 2. What happens today, and what goes wrong

*The current process as it is really done, and the specific point where it fails. One short
paragraph, concrete, no adjectives.*

## 3. Who is better off, and how you would know

*Name the people who benefit and the observable signal that would tell you it worked. Three
or four lines.*

## 4. What the system does, in outline

*The handful of things a user can do with it. A short list, one line each, verbs first.*

## 5. What it records

*The things the system stores and how they relate. A short list or a small table — not a
schema dump.*

## 6. In scope by the final week — and what is not

*Two lists. What will be working and demonstrable by the last week, and what you are
deliberately leaving out.*

## 7. After the semester

*What a future team or the client would do with this after you hand it over. Two or three
lines.*

## 8. What you told the client this is

*The words you actually used with the client about what they are getting — a student
project, its limits, and what happens at the end of the semester. Two or three lines.*
